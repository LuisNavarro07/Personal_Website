abstract: >-
  We explore the determinants of in-kind food provisions and financial donations during the COVID-19
  pandemic and find that both types of assistance dramatically increased at the onset of the pandemic.
  However, these increases were not persistent. We argue that this is evidence that the charitable
  food system serves a critical role in facilitating the transmission of federal provisions and
  charitable donations to households in need during times of crisis.
