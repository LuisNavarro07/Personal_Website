abstract: >-
  The "flypaper effect" is a widely documented anomaly in which subnational spending allocations grow
  faster when subsidized by an intergovernmental grant than when they are supported by own-source tax
  revenue raised from private income. Although numerous studies present empirical evidence of flypaper
  effects, others contradict those findings, for example by demonstrating that intergovernmental
  grants crowd-out the recipient's spending. This paper applies meta-analytic methods to test the
  flypaper effect in the U.S. context using 128 studies.
