title: "Liquidity and volatility in the municipal bond market: Evidence from the municipal liquidity facility and other early interventions"

publication: "*International Review of Economics & Finance*, 108, 105212"
doi: '10.1016/j.iref.2026.105212'
abstract: >-
  At the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic, the federal government stepped in with fiscal and monetary
  policy actions to assist distressed municipalities, including the Municipal Liquidity Facility
  (MLF), a market maker of last resort. We examine whether federal aid to states and the MLF restored
  proper functioning of the municipal bond market by estimating their effect on medium-term market
  volatility. Using a synthetic difference-in-differences approach that builds counterfactual
  volatility series from financial instruments outside the municipal market, we find that, for A-rated
  and higher-quality bonds, federal support reduced volatility by roughly 35% to 80% of the excess
  volatility state issuers experienced at the peak of the 2020 turmoil. Effects were strongest among
  higher-rated states, while direct fiscal aid was the main driver for lower-rated issuers. The
  results suggest the MLF helped reduce uncertainty and risk perception even though few states used
  it.
